Lasagna Recipe

Description

Make a classic meat lasagna by layering cooked lasagna noodles with a rich beef and sausage meat sauce, a creamy ricotta or cottage cheese mixture, and shredded mozzarella and parmesan. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 45 minutes until bubbly and golden, then rest 15 minutes before serving.

Ingredients

  • Meat sauce
  • Cheese layer
  • Noodles & Toppings

Steps

  1. Make the sauce: Brown the sausage, beef, onion, and garlic in a pot. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, tomato sauce, tomato paste, and seasonings. Simmer for 30 minutes to 1.5 hours
  2. Boil the noodles: Cook the lasagna noodles in salted boiling water until tender (8 to 10 minutes), then drain and rinse with cold water.
  3. Layer the pan: Spread meat sauce in a 9x13-inch baking dish. Add a layer of noodles, half the cheese mixture, mozzarella, meat sauce, and parmesan. Repeat the layers.
  4. Bake and rest: Cover with foil and bake at 375°F for 25 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for another 25 minutes. Let it rest for 15 minutes before cutting.
